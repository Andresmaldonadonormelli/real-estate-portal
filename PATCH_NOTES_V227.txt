V2.2.7 — Property Workspaces + Performance

WHAT'S NEW
- Properties now lead into a dedicated workspace for each individual property.
- New property sections: Overview, Performance, Units, Documents.
- Origin-inspired Performance page using the portal's existing category colors.
- Performance metrics: Gross Income, Operating Expenses, NOI, Cash Flow after mortgage, Operating Expense Ratio.
- Monthly Income vs Operating Expenses chart with NOI trend line.
- Color-coded operating expense breakdown with horizontal bars instead of a donut chart.
- Trend / watch section for maintenance spending, operating expense ratio, and cash flow.
- Overview shows rent roll, recent transactions, mortgage balance, occupancy and document count.
- Units and Documents are visible inside the individual property workspace.
- "View ledger" and document links now open Ledger & Docs already filtered to the property.

ACCOUNTING BEHAVIOR
- NOI excludes financing categories (mortgage principal, mortgage interest, unsplit mortgage), CapEx and owner distributions from operating expenses.
- Cash flow after mortgage uses all recorded cash expenses.
- Performance is based only on posted/non-declined transactions already in the portal. It does not invent property values, ROI or appreciation.
