RE Portal v2.2.25 - Property performance previews

- Redesigns Properties list into clickable performance-preview tiles inspired by the supplied reference.
- Whole tile opens the property; top-right chevron reinforces clickability.
- Removes list-level Open/Edit/+ Unit controls. Editing remains inside the property workflow.
- Shows occupancy, current monthly rent, YTD cash flow, and a compact property-health preview.
- Health bar uses green/yellow/orange/red based on occupancy, cash flow, and operating expense ratio.
- Fully vacant properties intentionally show an empty gray bar and "Performance pending" instead of a fake red performance score.
- Vacant properties show potential monthly rent based on unit rents.
- No SQL required.
