V2.2.14 — Mobile Width Fix

NO SQL REQUIRED.

WHAT WAS WRONG
The Performance page still had desktop/min-content widths leaking into the mobile layout.
The 620px chart was intended to scroll inside its chart area, but its grid/card parents were
allowed to use that width as their minimum width. That expanded the entire property page.

FIX
- Property Performance containers are now width:100%, max-width:100%, min-width:0 on mobile.
- The page itself cannot horizontally overflow.
- KPI grids remain two equal mobile columns without widening the page.
- The 620px chart remains readable and horizontally scrolls ONLY inside its own chart viewport.
- Operating Expenses breakdown is constrained to the card width.
- Long category labels truncate rather than force the card wider.
- Property tabs can scroll inside their own row if needed.

No functionality or database logic changed.
