V2.2.21 — Performance order, breakdown spacing, ledger date

1. Performance mobile hierarchy
- Period selector now sits directly above the chart.
- Chart moves before KPI cards on mobile.
- KPI cards follow the chart, then expense breakdown, watch, and trends.
- This makes YTD / Last 12M / year switching immediately visible.

2. Operating expense breakdown spacing
- Adds 8px between each category label row and its bar.
- Adds 8px between each bar and its percentage.
- Applies consistently to Repairs & Maintenance, Management Fee, Utilities, and future categories.

3. Cash Flow range controls
- No extra 3M / YTD / 1Y controls added.
- Cash Flow remains a focused trailing-12-month Monthly / Cumulative view.

4. Ledger mobile date placement
- Removes the detached date line on mobile.
- Date now sits at the end of the metadata line with property, unit and category.
- Desktop keeps its dedicated Date column.

No SQL / Supabase changes.
