V2.2.5 — Interactive Cash Flow + richer semantic color

- Adds Robinhood-style cash-flow inspection on touch/drag and desktop hover.
- Selected month gets a vertical guide line, emphasized point, month label, and value.
- Monthly mode exposes income, expenses, and net for the selected month.
- Cumulative mode shows cumulative value plus the selected month's net.
- Adds richer positive green, negative coral/red, and amber warning colors in both light and dark modes.
- Keeps brand mint separate from financial positive green.
- Dark mode moves closer to near-black with stronger typography contrast and more vivid data colors.
- No Supabase SQL required.
